Prototype with real gaze selection

TalkBridge gaze demo

The app suggests what a child means and the child chooses by looking at an answer and holding. The suggestions in this demo are scripted. The gaze selection uses your camera for real.

Scenario

Hold time to choose

1.5 s

Longer is safer for a child with involuntary movement. Shorter is faster.

For the tracking to work

  • Prop the device on a stand so it does not move.
  • Sit 30 to 50 cm away with light on your face, not behind you.
  • A tablet, a laptop or a phone held sideways works better than an upright phone, because the answers sit further apart.
  • Looking with your eyes or turning your head both work, as long as you do the same thing during setup.

Video is processed on this device and is not uploaded or saved. The face-tracking model is downloaded from Google when you start.
